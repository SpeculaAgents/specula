# Security

If you find a way to make the engine sign something false, take a payment it should not, serve data to someone who
did not pay for it, or reach anything on the engine that the site does not forward, please tell us privately first.

Open a private security advisory on this repository (Security, Report a vulnerability). Say what you did, what
happened and what you expected. We will answer, fix it, and credit you in the changelog if you want.

Please do not test against other people's wallets, and do not publish before a fix is out.
